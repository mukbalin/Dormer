Dormer policies

Privacy policy

This prototype explains how a production Dormer service would handle account and property information.

Information we collect

We collect information you submit when creating an account, adding a property, sending an inquiry, booking a room, or updating your profile. Verification information must be secured, access-controlled and retained only as needed.

How Dormer uses information

Your profile and preferences help personalise listings and roommate suggestions. Landlord data is used for verification and to help students communicate about a specific property.

Safety and reporting

Users can report a listing, message or review. A production system must provide human review, audit trails and a clear process for removing unsafe or inaccurate content.
